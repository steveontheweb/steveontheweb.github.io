About

I’m Steve Middleton. My work has centred on technical art, animation, tools, and production pipelines for games.

After years of building tools and pipelines for games, I’m increasingly focused on machine learning, AI evaluation, and safety. This site documents the technical path between those fields.

I’m interested in systems that sit between disciplines: the points where visual quality meets runtime constraints, model capability meets tooling, and useful autonomy meets the need for careful control.
